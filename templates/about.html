OUR MISSION

A little time can change a lot.

Voluntr was created to make community service accessible, meaningful and easier to discover.

Why we exist

Many people want to help but struggle to find a role that fits their interests, skills or schedule. Community organizations also need a simpler way to reach dependable volunteers. Voluntr brings these needs together in one place.

What we believe

Everyone has something to contribute. Whether you can give an afternoon, a weekend or a regular commitment, your time can matter. Good volunteering starts with listening, respecting community needs and following through.

How we build trust

Organizer listings are reviewed before publication. Volunteers should confirm arrangements, safeguarding requirements and accessibility with the host before attending. Listing review is not a background check or guarantee of safety.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous experience?

Many roles welcome beginners. Check the specific skills and training requirements for each listing.

Can I volunteer remotely?

Yes. Use the format filter to discover remote or hybrid opportunities.

How do I become an organizer?

Create an organizer account, submit a listing and wait for administrator review.

How are hours recorded?

Accepted volunteers submit hours, which the listing organizer can approve.
