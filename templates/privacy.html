Privacy overview

Voluntr stores account details, profile information, applications, bookmarks and hours to operate the platform. Organizers can see applications and hour records for their own listings. Administrators can access records to manage the platform.

Newsletter email addresses and contact messages are stored separately. This version does not send automated marketing email. Essential session and CSRF cookies support authentication and security.

Use the contact page to request access, correction or deletion of your information. The operator must establish a retention and deletion process and finalize applicable privacy terms before public launch.
